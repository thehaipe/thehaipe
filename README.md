# 💫 About Me:
### iOS R&D Engineer @ Buffertino

I build native Apple-platform products with a focus on SwiftUI, product engineering, and polished user experiences.

I worked on **Ravelo: Screen Time Banking** as an iOS R&D engineer, with a strong focus on the iOS app experience. Ravelo is a focus and screen-time product for people who want more intentional control over distracting apps. My work centered on native iOS development, Screen Time APIs, app extensions, subscriptions, analytics, localization, onboarding, and high-quality SwiftUI interfaces and retention mechanics.

I also worked on **Bufferly**, a native macOS clipboard manager built in Swift. Bufferly improves the default clipboard with a visual history, app-based grouping, pinned items, Quick Look previews, notes, sharing, and fast keyboard-first workflows.

#### Highlights

- Native iOS and macOS development with Swift and SwiftUI
- Experience with Apple Screen Time APIs, WidgetKit, Live Activities, AppIntents, and app extensions
- Product-focused R&D across onboarding, retention, subscriptions, analytics, and premium UX
- Integration experience with tools such as RevenueCat and PostHog
- Strong attention to interaction design, accessibility-minded flows, localization, and App Store-ready polish
